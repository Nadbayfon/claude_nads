This is the daily-use guide for the four planners. One page per
surface, plus role-specific cheat-sheets at the end.

## You have three ways to ask the AI

| If you're at… | Use… | What's nice about it |
|---|---|---|
| Your laptop, in the workspace | The chat panel on the right | Drag in PDFs / paste text; see your other planning data in the same screen |
| Your phone, on the go | WhatsApp message to Crystal Events' business number | Voice notes work; AI replies with a digest you can act on |
| Your inbox | BCC `ai@crystalevents.eu` on any email, or forward to it | The AI files the email to the right couple's comm log + drafts a reply in your own sent folder |

All three share **your** conversation with the AI. Switching surface
mid-thought works.

## Your conversation is private

Other planners can't see your chat with the AI by default. When you
want to share something — a summary, a proposed RFQ, a meeting recap —
click "share to team" in the chat panel, or text `/share` on WhatsApp.

You can also "save to comm log" to file the AI's output as part of the
wedding's official correspondence.

## Connecting your accounts

Settings → My identities:

- **Google account / Microsoft account** for email drafting in your
  own inbox.
- **Calendar** (same OAuth) for "schedule a meeting Wed PM" → it lands
  in your calendar.
- **Phone number** so the AI knows it's you on WhatsApp.

If a connection breaks (you change password, revoke access, etc.) the
AI will tell you and prompt to reconnect.

## Common things to ask

- "What's [Couple]'s next deadline?"
- "Draft an RFQ to La Floreria for Sham & Shwan, Catalan, June wedding."
- "Read this PDF" (drop a provider proposal).
- "Compare the three caterers we've shortlisted for Sham & Shwan."
- "Schedule a venue visit Wednesday afternoon."
- "Send me the next-2-hours timeline for tomorrow's wedding at 09:00."

## Things the AI will refuse

- Posting anything to social media (we don't, by policy).
- Sending an email or WhatsApp without your explicit confirmation.
- Acting on an unknown WhatsApp number without confirming first.
- Including a couple's surname in any provider-facing draft.
- Confirming a Roc 35 booking on a Núria-led wedding without Jennifer's
  acknowledgement.

## Cheat-sheets per role

### Jennifer (owner)
- Dashboard shows all weddings, COI flags, and the team's AI-bill.
- You're the only role that can acknowledge COI.
- Wedding-day digests come at 09:00 the day before + every 2h on the day.

### Núria (planner)
- You see only the weddings you're assigned to.
- COI banner appears on any of your weddings where Roc 35 is in play.

  automatically to the proposal.

### Róisín (stylist)
- Read access to assigned weddings; write on seating, briefs, visit
  agendas.
- AI is good at "draw me a tablescape mood board prompt" — you pair
  the AI text output with your image library.

### Jackie (admin)
- Full write access to providers, comm logs, and payment milestones.
- Best WhatsApp use: voice-note-as-you-walk for follow-up errands; the
  AI files it to the right wedding.

## When something feels wrong

- **Wrong wedding context**: the AI sometimes can't tell which wedding
  you mean. Just say "for Sham & Shwan" and it remembers for the rest
  of the conversation.
- **AI slow to reply on WhatsApp**: heavy operations queue up and run
  in the background; the AI sends a "I'll get back to you in a couple
  of minutes" auto-acknowledgement, then the result.
- **Weird output**: click the 👎 in the chat panel (or text `bad
  reply` on WhatsApp) and write what you expected. This goes straight
  into the eval set.
